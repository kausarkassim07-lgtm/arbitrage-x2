How it works.

The idea is simple. If a trip through a few currencies brings you back with more than you started with, that gap is an opportunity.

The big idea

Swap money from one currency into another, and keep going until you are back where you began. Normally you end up with a little less. But when rates drift out of line, you can end up with more, and that extra is the opportunity.

Following 1,000 USD once around the loop.

Watch the search happen

The Floyd–Warshall method checks every currency in turn as a possible stepping stone. Each cell shows the best score for going from the row currency to the column currency. Press Next step and watch the numbers improve. If any cell on the diagonal ends up negative, a money-making loop may exist.

Each cell is the score of going straight from the row currency to the column currency. Lower is better. Press Next step.
